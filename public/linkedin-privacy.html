Privacy Policy

VT Security · LinkedIn integration

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Who operates it

The VT Security LinkedIn integration (the LinkedIn developer applications VT Commentary Media and VT Security Media) is operated by Valentin Torassa Colombero, an independent professional based in Santa Fe, Argentina. It is an internal tool: it is not offered to the public, has no customers, and manages no LinkedIn accounts other than the operator's own member profile and the LinkedIn Pages that the operator administers.

What it does

It publishes posts that the operator has written and explicitly approved, and it reads the performance of those posts (impressions, reactions, comments, follower counts) so editorial decisions can be based on measured results. Nothing is published automatically: every post requires a manual approval step.

LinkedIn data it accesses

  • Basic identity of the authorizing member (name, member ID, email) — to know which profile the token belongs to.
  • Posts, comments and reactions created by that member or by Pages that member administers.
  • Aggregate analytics for those posts and Pages (impressions, clicks, reactions, follower counts).

It does not access other members' profiles, connections, messages, or any data about people beyond what LinkedIn includes in aggregate post and Page statistics.

Where that data goes

Data is fetched from the LinkedIn API over TLS and stored on a private server operated by the operator, together with analytics from the operator's other own channels. It is never uploaded to any third-party service, and the OAuth token is stored on that server with owner-only file permissions.

What it never does

  • It does not sell, rent, or share LinkedIn data with anyone.
  • It does not use LinkedIn data for advertising, sales prospecting, or lead generation.
  • It does not use LinkedIn data to train any AI or machine-learning model.
  • It does not combine LinkedIn data with data from other members or build profiles of people.
  • It does not post, comment, or react on behalf of anyone other than the operator.

Retention and deletion

Post and Page analytics are kept only as long as they are useful for the operator's own editorial reporting. All data obtained through the LinkedIn API can be deleted on request, including at LinkedIn's request, by removing the corresponding records and the stored token from the private server. The authorizing member can revoke the integration's access at any time from LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services.

Privacy compliance

Valentin Torassa Colombero is responsible for this integration's compliance with applicable privacy regulations, including Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Ley 25.326), and with the LinkedIn API Terms of Use.
