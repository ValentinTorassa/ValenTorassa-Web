Privacy Policy

VT CreatorStack

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Operator and purpose

VT CreatorStack is an internal YouTube publishing and analytics workspace operated by Valentin Torassa Colombero in Argentina. It is used for channels the operator owns or administers. It is not offered as a public service.

Google and YouTube data accessed

When the operator connects a YouTube channel, the application requests access to read channel and video information, upload and manage videos, manage channel interactions where enabled, and read YouTube Analytics. It can retrieve video metadata, channel statistics, comments, and analytics returned by the YouTube APIs for that channel.

Use and storage

The data is used to display channel status and analytics and to carry out actions initiated in the operator's workspace. OAuth credentials and application data are kept on the operator's private server, which is backed up by the operator. Data is not sold or shared for advertising, and YouTube data is not used to train AI models.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

The operator retains channel data and OAuth credentials while the integration is in use and may delete them from the private server and its backups. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future API access; stored copies must also be deleted separately. To request deletion or ask about the data, contact valentintorassa21@gmail.com.

Platform terms

This integration uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
