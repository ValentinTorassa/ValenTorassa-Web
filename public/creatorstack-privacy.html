Privacy Policy

VT CreatorStack

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Operator and purpose

VT CreatorStack is an internal YouTube publishing and analytics workspace operated by Valentin Torassa Colombero in Argentina. It is used for channels the operator owns or administers. It is not offered as a public service.

Google and YouTube data accessed

When the operator connects a YouTube channel, the application asks Google for these permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: read channel, playlist and video information.
  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the connected channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: manage the channel's videos, playlists, captions and comments.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read YouTube Analytics reports for the channel.

With them it can retrieve video metadata, channel statistics, comments and the analytics the YouTube APIs return for that channel. It does not request the operator's Google contacts, email, or data from other Google services.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show channel status and analytics in the operator's workspace and to carry out actions the operator starts there, such as uploading or scheduling a video. It is not sold, not shared for advertising, not transferred to third parties, and YouTube data is not used to train AI models.

VT CreatorStack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and information on your device

OAuth credentials and application data are kept on the operator's private server and in the operator's backups of that server.

The workspace's web interface stores its settings, such as the OAuth client ID, and cached interface data in the browser's local storage on the operator's own device. VT CreatorStack does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not let third parties store or read information on the device.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

Channel data and OAuth credentials are kept while the integration is in use. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Within 7 calendar days of a revocation or a deletion request, the operator deletes the stored OAuth credentials and the YouTube data obtained through them from the private server. Backup copies are never restored for use after that and expire as the backups rotate, within 90 days.
